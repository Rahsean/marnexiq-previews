About Our Founder

Advocate, Mentor, Social Change Agent, & Educator

Welcome to the inspiring journey of Charlotte Garnes, the visionary founder behind ReNforce. Charlotte's personal experiences with workplace discrimination following her incarceration served as the catalyst for ReNforce's creation.

With a solid educational background, holding both bachelor's and master's degrees, Charlotte unexpectedly found herself jobless after her employer discovered her legal conviction. This setback led Charlotte to commit herself to making a difference, and she established ReNforce.

Charlotte is a certified Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion specialist, earning her DEI certificate from the University of South Florida. Furthermore, she is a Licensed Mental Health Clinician Associate in North Carolina. Charlotte's expertise and passion drive ReNforce's efforts to empower individuals, challenge societal norms, and create opportunities for a brighter future.
