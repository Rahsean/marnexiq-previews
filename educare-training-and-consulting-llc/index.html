Hey friend, welcome in

Burnout almost cost me everything.

You've given your all — blood, sweat, tears, and most importantly your heart. The problem is you've been so busy pouring into everyone else's cup that there's nothing left in your own.

You're successful, accomplished, a decade or more of experience behind you. And all of it is starting to feel like it doesn't matter. Even though no one else sees it, I do — because I've been there.

What you need isn't more work. You need a mirror: to see how to maximise your strengths, change your internal dialogue, and minimise burnout. That's where I come in.
