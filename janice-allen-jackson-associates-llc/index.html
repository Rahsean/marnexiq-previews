Biography

Janice Allen Jackson

Mrs. Jackson has served as City Manager of Albany, GA, and Administrator of the consolidated Augusta, GA government. She also worked as one of four General Managers reporting to the County Manager in Mecklenburg County, NC, and Assistant City Manager in Albany.

Throughout her career, she has always enjoyed bringing major projects to fruition, building public trust in government, and empowering staff to become their best and most productive. Her unique talents revolve around her willingness to address issues head on with honesty, sincerity, and integrity.

She has been active in the United Way organizations in Augusta, Albany, and the Central Carolinas, the International and Georgia City-County Manager's Associations, Women's National Book Association, and the National Forum for Black Public Administrators. She is a graduate of Leadership Georgia, Leadership Albany, Leadership Charlotte, and Leadership Augusta.

A Diamond Life member of the Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc., and an active Rotarian/Paul Harris Fellow, she graduated from the Academy Of Richmond County and was the first student at the College of William and Mary to pursue the interdisciplinary major in Public Policy, completing it with a concentration in Urban Policy. She later earned a Master's Degree in Public Policy with a concentration in Management from Duke University.

What We Believe

Our firm, and our work are shaped by the following principles:

  • There IS a better way, and we will find it! It is our job to meet you where you are, and get you to where you need to be.
  • What is best for our end users/customers is our primary consideration; we do not exist without them.
  • Gimmicks are no substitute for a thoughtful and sincere approach; simplicity in communication and process is desirable.
  • Acting with personal and professional integrity is the best way to build confidence among people having personal and professional integrity.
