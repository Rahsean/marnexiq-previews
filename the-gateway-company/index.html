Our Story

Our Heart Is to Be Part of Your Team

After ten years of writing grants for a nonprofit near Augusta, Georgia, that serves children with special needs, the founder brought on Ashley to increase the number of grants submitted. Our complementary backgrounds in prose and technical writing made us a strong team, and we actually enjoyed taking on all the grants.

We realized our skill sets could potentially help other nonprofits. So in February of 2017, Gateway Grant Services was born.

Not long after we launched, we discovered that our clients needed help with development - for their program, their budgets, their board. Coaching our clients through these foundational elements, crucial for competitive grants, gave us other tools to aid in building their sustainability.

As we watched our clients face the financial impact of COVID-19, we responded to their needs by launching our Virtual Fundraising & Digital Marketing services in the fall of 2020. Similarly, we watched small businesses struggle and branched into small business coaching in the summer of 2020.
