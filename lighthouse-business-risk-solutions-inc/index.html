The problem with clean books

Financial strategy is complex — but your plan shouldn't be reactive.

Even “simple” financial statements can hide cash flow leaks, pricing problems and missed opportunities that impact profitability. Software can track transactions, but it can't think like a CFO.

That's why our best work happens in an ongoing monthly relationship — so we can stay close to your numbers year-round, connect the dots to your goals, and help you reduce surprises, improve cash flow, and make confident decisions in every season of business.

We go beyond clean books with clear monthly financial reporting, cash flow visibility and planning, and pricing and profitability analysis, plus ongoing decision support built around your goals, not generic reports.

Our CPA & Founder

Michelle L. Anthony, CPA

Michelle founded Lighthouse after 30+ years in accounting and business, having seen the same pattern over and over: owners working hard, making money, and still feeling unsure about what their numbers meant, or what to do next. Lighthouse was built to change that.

She has worked across construction, retail, hospitality and real estate, so she understands the day-to-day realities behind the financials — cash flow swings, payroll pressure, pricing decisions, and the constant need to make smart calls with incomplete information. With a large portion of clients being real estate investors, she brings added insight from her own real estate background, including an inactive real estate salesperson license, an inactive real estate appraiser license and years of experience as a former property manager.

Michelle earned her MBA with a concentration in Accounting, successfully completed the Uniform CPA Exam, and is a licensed CPA in the state of Georgia.
