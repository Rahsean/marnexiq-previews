Who We Are

Hotel Management Expertise

SIG is a privately held real estate management firm based in Augusta, Georgia. Our company's portfolio includes hotels ranging from limited service to full service properties operating under major hospitality brands including Hilton, Marriott, IHG, and Choice flags.

With over 1,700 managed guest rooms, SIG has established itself as a proven leader in hotel management. Our experienced management team brings decades of combined expertise in hotel operations, development, and strategic planning.

Our Mission

SIG's mission is to implement efficient operational strategies that maximize financial performance in order to subsequently result in company growth. This objective is achieved through three key areas of focus that drive success across our entire portfolio.
