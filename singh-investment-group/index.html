Our Growth Story

1997
SIG builds its first hotel, Wingate Inn.
2002
SIG begins new partnership with Hilton Worldwide, opening a Hampton Inn & Suites.
2005
SIG opens its first full-service hotel at entrance of Fort Gordon.
2007
A corporate restructuring establishes new hotel management and development divisions.
2008
Hilton Garden Inn opens and becomes RevPAR market leader within one month.
2009
SIG becomes a franchisee of Starwood.
2010
SIG begins three-hotel redevelopment near entrance of Augusta National Golf Club.
2011
SIG hires its 500th Employee; repositions the DoubleTree Riverfront in New Bern, NC.
2013
Sheraton Augusta wins Starwood Hotel of the Year Award (worldwide).
2014
SIG repositions foreclosed Atlantic Beach, NC oceanfront resort into DoubleTree and wins Conversion of the Year (worldwide); employs approximately 750 team members.
2018
SIG opens two Hilton branded hotels – Homewood Suites and Home2 Suites.
2020
SIG opens SpringHill Suites by Marriott Hilton Head Island.
