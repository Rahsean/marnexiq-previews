Our Growth Story

Since 1997
1997

SIG builds its first hotel, Wingate Inn.

2002

SIG begins new partnership with Hilton Worldwide, opening a Hampton Inn & Suites.

2005

SIG opens its first full-service hotel at the entrance of Fort Gordon.

2007

A corporate restructuring establishes dedicated hotel management and development divisions.

2008

Hilton Garden Inn opens and becomes RevPAR market leader within one month.

2009

SIG becomes a franchisee of Starwood.

2010

SIG begins a three-hotel redevelopment near the entrance of Augusta National Golf Club.

2011

SIG hires its 500th employee and repositions the DoubleTree Riverfront in New Bern, NC.

2013

Sheraton Augusta wins Starwood Hotel of the Year Award (worldwide).

2014

SIG repositions a foreclosed Atlantic Beach, NC oceanfront resort into a DoubleTree and wins Conversion of the Year (worldwide). SIG employs approximately 750 team members.

2018

SIG opens two Hilton-branded hotels, Homewood Suites and Home2 Suites.

2020

SIG opens SpringHill Suites by Marriott Hilton Head Island.
