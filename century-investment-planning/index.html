Our History

Six decades of trusted financial guidance

1961

The firm's history began when its founder, after completing service in the U.S. Army, became a Certified Public Accountant and began working in his hometown of Augusta, Georgia.

1994

Money Advisors, Inc. was established out of necessity for tax clients who needed strategic investment planning services, combining CPA expertise with comprehensive financial planning.

2017

After retiring as Managing Partner of a CPA firm, the founder continued with financial planning and maintained the investment advisory practice. Century Investment Planning was formed to identify Money Advisors, Inc's core business operation.
