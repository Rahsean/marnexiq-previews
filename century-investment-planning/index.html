Independent Registered Investment Advisors

Guidance. Growth. Protection. Legacy.

As Independent Registered Investment Advisors, we have a fiduciary responsibility to act in our client's best interest, not ours. We nurture long-term partnerships with all our clients through dedicated personal service and strategic investment planning.

Schedule Consultation
01

Financial Analysis

We collaborate and review the full scope of your financial wellbeing and establish personal forecasts that help guide you through a successful financial future. Your investment holdings may include businesses, partnerships, real estate, stocks, bonds, and annuities. We evaluate your cash flow, assets, income and tax liabilities while discussing your short term priorities and long term goals.

02

Portfolio Management

We construct unique portfolio strategies aligned with your risk tolerance and objectives for wealth accumulation. Our investment selection process is based on extensive fundamental research to assess each investment's underlying financial stability and future earnings potential. We proactively monitor and rebalance investment allocations as your objectives evolve.

03

Retirement Solutions

We analyze your path to retirement and advise on protecting your financial independence and long-term care. Early planning and maximizing retirement investments at the beginning of a career can lay the foundation for lifelong savings habits necessary for funding businesses, college, special events and cash flow needs in retirement.

04

Estate Planning

We coordinate with you to ensure your estate is in order. We support you in outlining details for wills, trusts, powers of attorney, charitable endowments and other directives to ensure your lasting legacy. We help establish direct beneficiaries for as many assets as possible to avoid probate.

Our Foundation

Committed. Independent. Fiduciaries.

Our firm's history began in 1961 when the founder completed service in the U.S. Army, became a Certified Public Accountant and began working in Augusta, Georgia. Money Advisors, Inc. was established in 1994 out of necessity for many tax clients in need of strategic investment planning services.

In 2017, Century Investment Planning was formed to identify Money Advisors, Inc's core business operation. Our growth has been exclusively based on the referrals made by our existing clients. We will not compromise our ability to provide personal service and attention to our clients.

Our Approach

Long-term Investment Philosophy

01

Fundamental Research

Our investment selection process is based on extensive fundamental research to assess each investment's underlying financial stability and future earnings potential. We have access to numerous professional resources that help us analyze and identify high quality investments.

02

Long-term Time Horizon

We strongly encourage early planning and long term investing. A long term time horizon historically shows investment growth overcomes short term crisis, economic fluctuations and market volatility. Time is a major factor that supports wealth accumulation.

03

Asset Custody

Client assets are held by Charles Schwab & Co, Inc., which acts as the custodian of our accounts under management. Schwab's Advisor Services platform assists in processing administrative services and issues transaction statements directly to clients on a monthly basis.
