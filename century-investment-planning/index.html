Our Foundation

Built on Trust and Independence

1961

Committed. Independent. Fiduciaries.

Our firm's history began in 1961 when our founder, after completing service in the U.S. Army, became a Certified Public Accountant and began working in Augusta, Georgia. Money Advisors, Inc. was established in 1994 out of necessity for many tax clients in need of strategic investment planning services.

As Independent Registered Investment Advisors, we have a fiduciary responsibility to act in our client's best interest, not ours. Our growth has been exclusively based on the referrals made by our existing clients. We will not compromise our ability to provide personal service and attention.

Our Approach

Long-Term Investment Philosophy

We strongly encourage early planning and long-term investing. A long-term time horizon historically shows investment growth overcomes short-term crisis, economic fluctuations and market volatility. This approach allows for maximum growth potential and the flexibility to adjust strategies that align with all your lifetime milestones.
