About AV3

Small Native American and Veteran Owned

Since 2006, AV3 Inc. has been a small Native American and Veteran owned Audio-Visual Systems Integration company headquartered in Southern Maryland. We provide products and services centered on Audio-Visual integration and installation, using a turnkey approach for customer requirements and challenges.

Our team of expert engineers and technicians has exceeded customer expectations and remains a cutting edge AV and multimedia systems organization. From rapid response to customer requests, to in-house systems configuration and onsite installation, through maintenance - the dedication and expertise of our experienced workforce sets us apart.
