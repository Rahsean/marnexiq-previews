Web Development

Worthouse

The Challenge

A metal roof manufacturing company whose old site was managed by a team in Poland. It got the job done but they wanted something that told a visual story and worked seamlessly on mobile devices.

What We Did

As a roofing manufacturer, they needed visually stunning assets to showcase flagship metal roofing products. We took a bare-boned 65+ page website, refreshed the template and built an easily navigable, fully optimized site with a custom mobile menu.

The Outcome

A completely transformed digital presence that effectively showcases their products across all devices, with improved navigation and user experience that better serves their customers and drives conversions.

Web Development & Infrastructure

The Augusta Press

The Challenge

A local online newspaper that needed a team on call in case something catastrophic happened to their website. With a large number of subscribers, there is no room for downtime.

What We Did

They needed a web designer who also understood payment processors and larger digital infrastructure. The work involved migrating between two different hosts, redesigning the site into a more dynamic approach, and creating a comprehensive support system.

The Outcome

A robust, reliable platform that handles their subscriber base with zero downtime, removing the burden of dedicated in-house technical staff while ensuring their content delivery remains uninterrupted.
