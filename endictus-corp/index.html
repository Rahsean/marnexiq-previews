About ENDICTUS

ENDICTUS Corp. was founded in 2017 out of a desire to bring capable, yet highly nimble and responsive program management services to the Department of Defense (DoD) and Intelligence Community (IC), and other government customers.

This core commitment to readiness and responsiveness resulted in continual and steady customer-driven growth to bring the ENDICTUS brand of service to other areas of support beyond program management. Today ENDICTUS offers services across 4 core competencies: OSINT / Privacy, Cybersecurity, IT Modernization, and Professional Services.

From an operational support perspective, ENDICTUS leverages the domain knowledge and technical experience of our ex-uniformed cyber operators and testers, as well as the decades of specialized experience from our cyber industry experts, to deliver mission-focused and operationally-stable solutions to the DoD and Intelligence Communities across our 4 core competencies.
