About Departure Media

A Dynamic and Collaborative ACDBE Prime Operator

Departure Media is a dynamic and collaborative ACDBE Prime operator that manages advertising concessions for airports throughout the United States. We are proud to be 100% woman-owned, and the largest out-of-home advertising company in the country that focuses exclusively on airports.

We unite industry experience, creativity, and innovation to draw the attention of passing travelers and build brand awareness while enhancing the overall travel experience. Departure Media's advertising programs complement the distinct aesthetic and sense of place of each individual airport we serve.

Our History

Leslie Bensen, our President & CEO, founded Departure Media in 1996. After spending over 10 years managing terminal advertising concessions for a large national firm, Leslie saw an opportunity to do something different—something better. Departure Media was created to be a "departure from the norm" of airport advertising, offering improved customer service and ongoing attention to detail with a creative approach.

Departure Media has grown to encompass a full and ever-expanding team of talented professionals, partnering with countless airports throughout the United States. Today, we remain a 100% woman-owned company, motivated by our mission of being the airport advertising industry leader by building and maintaining relationships for life.
