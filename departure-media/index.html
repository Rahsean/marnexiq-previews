WHO WE ARE

The largest airport advertising company in the country

Departure Media is a dynamic and collaborative ACDBE Prime operator that manages advertising concessions for airports throughout the United States. We are proud to be 100% woman-owned, and the largest out-of-home advertising company in the country that focuses exclusively on airports.

Founded in 1996, Departure Media was created to be a "departure from the norm" of airport advertising, offering improved customer service and ongoing attention to detail with a creative approach. Our leadership team has more than 30 years of experience working with airports specifically on the transition, new construction, renovation, and custom design of terminal-wide advertising display programs.
