About Us

How We Learned to Strengthen Prayer Life, Grow Ministries, and Increase Financial Giving

In 1996, CSC Co-founders Eric and Lisa McArdle attended the National Catholic Stewardship Council Conference, where they spoke with pastors who recognized the importance of stewardship for growing disciples. Inspired by their commitment to their parishes, they left the conference resolved to help.

At the request of their pastor and alongside co-founder Deacon Don McArdle, they began spreading the message of stewardship in our home parish—Church of the Most Holy Trinity in Augusta, GA. Through communication, education, and evangelization, the McArdles facilitated their parish's first Stewardship Renewal and conducted a capital campaign.

In that first year, parishioners generated an offertory increase of over $100,000 in their small, inner-city parish and raised funds to restore their 150-year-old church. More importantly, ministries flourished, Mass attendance grew, and the overall spiritual energy soared.

Over almost three decades, the McArdles and CSC have helped hundreds of diverse Catholic parishes across the country in over 70 dioceses. At CSC, we provide services to strengthen prayer life, engage parishioners, and increase financial giving. We also train parish leaders to meet the needs of parishioners while helping them grow as disciples of Christ.
