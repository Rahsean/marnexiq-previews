01 — Our story

How we got into
the wedding business.

When I was a teenager, my daddy built a barn and pastures behind the house that I grew up in, and bought 3 Tennessee Walking horses for bird hunting. I don't know if he ever hunted from the back of those horses. He passed away in 1995.

In 2001 I bought my youngest daughter a Premarin foal from Canada — I told myself I was not really buying her a horse as much as rescuing one from certain death. A few years later my sister gave me 3 Angora goats to raise wool for her to make into rugs. We began rescuing horses, and wanted to raise and grow our own food. The farm grew to about 15 Highlander cows, 9 Angus cows, 15 horses, chickens, a mule, and 2 donkeys.

In 2013 a minister came to the farm to see about boarding his horse. We negotiated a price, and right before he left he asked if he could pray for me. He took my hands and said the sweetest prayer. Two weeks later we had a terrible fire, and the barn burned down with a total loss.

God always has a plan.
God turned ashes into beauty.

When we started to rebuild, I decided to build a hay and equipment barn. My daughter was recently engaged and wanted to have a dance, so I thought we might use the hay barn for her wedding. We had the barn landscaped, and it was a beautiful wedding. After rebuilding Daddy's barn, my daughter advertised it for rentals on Facebook — and I received so many calls that I was booked up for 2015 almost right away.

That minister still boards his horse with me, and now he drives our horse and carriage. We all love this business, and are so thankful for all the couples choosing to get married at our Kelly Chapel.

02 — The spaces

Six of them, all on one farm.

A ceremony, a dinner, a dance and a place to sleep — all on the property.

The Kelly Chapel

Ceremony

A quaint open chapel in the pasture across from the Magnolia Barn, with seating for around 190 people on pews under cover — and room to accommodate more around the chapel while keeping visibility of the ceremony. There are 29 wooden pews inside and 14 outside, each seating 6–7. The stage at the front has plenty of room for a large wedding party, and the chapel is equipped with a sound system: two lapel microphones and one handheld with a stand. Plastic wall covering can be used in case of inclement weather, decided three days in advance. Don't forget to ring the bell when you say "I do."

The Magnolia Barn

Reception & dancing

A portable stage, plenty of room for a dance floor and guest seating, and a large grassy courtyard at the front for tables or food stations. Behind the barn there is a fire pit surrounded by porch swings, and parking sits in a large pasture around it. Assorted tables and chairs are included — 48-inch round teak tables seating 4–6, 36-inch rounds seating 2–4, wooden bar tops, and 8- and 6-foot tables — along with two old rustic tables and a movable bar. The bath cabin is handicap accessible and a commercial kitchen is available. It suits corporate events and very large parties too.

The Highlands Room

Up to 56 guests

Adjacent to the commercial kitchen, in the same building as the Magnolia Barn and Bridal Suite. A versatile, inviting space designed to comfortably accommodate up to 56 guests, with flexibility for additional seating — ideal for rehearsal dinners, bridal luncheons, baby and bridal showers, intimate family gatherings and corporate dinners.

Sweetdaddy's Barn

Rehearsal dinners

The main working barn. Climate-controlled, with a kitchen holding a large stovetop and oven and a large refrigerator. Upstairs there is a large loft with an antique round window overlooking the horse pastures and a balcony over the cattle pastures — right for a rehearsal dinner, and a gathering place for the groom and his groomsmen on the day. The loft holds 50 chairs and assorted tables. The front lawn makes a gorgeous backdrop for a ceremony, with a large 18th-century church door brought from England.

The Bridal Suite

Getting ready

Available on the day of the wedding for the bride and her bridesmaids to dress. Furnished with couches and armchairs, stations for hair and makeup, a refrigerator, and a full bath including a tub.

The Cabins

Eight rooms, four cabins

Eight beautiful rustic rooms across four cabins, available as rentals. Each room includes a king-sized bed, a roll-away or day bed, a shower, a makeup table, a walk-in closet, a small coffee pot with accessories, towels and sheets. Cabin rentals are open to the public, so book the full package or reserve your rooms early to be sure of them on your weekend.
