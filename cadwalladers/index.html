01 — Our story

Carrying on
the tradition.

Located at 106-A Davis Road in Martinez, Georgia — a mere four miles from the Augusta National Golf Course, home of the Masters Golf Tournament — Cadwalladers has been an upscale dining establishment since 1987.

It was owned and operated by its previous owners until the end of 2020. Cadwalladers is now under new ownership, honored to carry on that tradition of fine food, fine wines and atmosphere — by an owner who worked with Cadwalladers for over five years before purchasing the business.

You can usually catch the owner greeting you at the door on arrival.
